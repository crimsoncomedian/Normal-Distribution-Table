# Normal Distribution Table

Two kinds of question that alternate. In Fill the table, students get a real-world setting with a mean and standard deviation and type the value at z = −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2 and 3, under a bell curve with a column for each z-score. In Find the mean or SD, three values from the table are shown and students work out the missing mean or standard deviation. Hints are short plain-text nudges that point back to the curve, and wrong answers get feedback for common slips. Choose Mixed, Fill the table, or Find mean or SD. First-try score and streak are kept on the page only.

Open `index.html` in a browser, no build step needed.
